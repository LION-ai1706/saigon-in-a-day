01 · Problem

Short-stay visitors plan their one day in Saigon from scattered, often outdated information

Scattered sources

Visitors who stay one or two days piece together a route from blogs, social posts and general-purpose AI chatbots.

Wrong or outdated details

Opening hours, prices and closures change. Chatbots can confidently suggest places that are closed or do not exist.

Lost time, same few sites

Routes cut across heavy traffic, and visitors default to the same famous landmarks. Smaller museums and local experiences are rarely found.

Real examples from our own data research (Nov 2026): the Ho Chi Minh Museum branch at Nhà Rồng has been closed for renovation since 7 May 2026; the Notre-Dame Cathedral interior was reported closed for restoration; and four city museums changed their adult fee to 60,000 VND from 1 October 2026, while older guides still show other hours and prices.

03 · Solution

A timed one-day plan built only from places we have verified

1

Ask. Interests, budget, hotel area, date and time window, mobility, group type, optional evening.

2

Select. Draw only from a curated database built from official public sources. Nothing outside it is ever suggested.

3

Order. Arrange stops by location, opening hours and travel time into a timed plan with a lunch window.

4

Show. English guidance plus Vietnamese names and addresses on a "show to driver" card.

5

Correct. Visitors report wrong information; we check and fix the database.

Trust by design

  • Every stop shows its source link and a "last verified" date.
  • Warnings for closed days, renovations and conflicting sources.
  • When we do not have a verified answer, the assistant says so instead of guessing.
  • A deterministic planning engine does the work. An AI layer is optional and is never allowed to add places, prices or hours.

07 · Business model

Free for travellers. Hotels and tour operators pay.

Travellers

Free

The full planner, driver cards and sources at no cost. Trust is the product; no paywall between a visitor and correct information.

Main revenue

B2B white-label plans

Hotels and tour operators offer the planner to guests under their own brand, by QR code at reception or in pre-arrival messages.

Secondary

Featured listings

Verified local businesses can pay for visibility, always clearly labelled as featured and never ranked above the visitor's own needs.

Later

Anonymised insights

Aggregate trends on visitor interests for partners. No personal data collected or sold.

First step: pilot with 3–5 hotels

Offer the planner free during the pilot, measure guest use and satisfaction, then agree a paid plan with the hotels that see value.
